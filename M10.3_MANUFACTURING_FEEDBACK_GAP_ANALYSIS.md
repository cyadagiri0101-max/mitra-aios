## 1. Analyzed Capabilities & Closed Gaps

1. **Raw Telemetry Ingestion:** Implemented `ManufacturingSignal` supporting high-frequency sensor feeds (Cycle time, RPM, Temperature, Vibration).
2. **Deterministic Data Quality:** Quarantines out-of-range, malformed, late, or duplicate signals.
3. **EKOS Lineage Mapping:** Automatically projects observations into `ekos_graph_nodes` linked to machines, work orders, and projects via `OBSERVED_IN` edges.
