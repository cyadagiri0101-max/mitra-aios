# M10.2 — G12 Knowledge Governance Integration Specification
## Connecting Knowledge Articles & Evidence to the EKOS Graph
**Milestone:** `M10.2`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. G12 Graph Integration Points

- `KnowledgeArticle` entities map directly to `ekos_graph_nodes` with `entity_type = 'KNOWLEDGE_ARTICLE'`.
- Article generation from trial observations or NCRs emits `CITED_BY` / `RESOLVED_BY` edges.
- Peer review and publication events update the node's lifecycle metadata in the graph projection.
