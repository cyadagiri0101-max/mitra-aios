## 1. Audit Events Ledger

- `GEOMETRIC_FEATURES_EXTRACTED`: Emitted upon canonical feature extraction and unit normalization.
- `DFM_RULES_EVALUATED`: Emitted upon deterministic rule evaluation.
- `DFM_FINDING_REVIEWED`: Emitted when an engineer accepts, modifies, or rejects a finding.
