## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone **M8.5** bridges governed institutional knowledge (`KnowledgeArticle`) with certified engineering retrieval evidence (`KnowledgeChunk` / G13).

- **`KnowledgeChunk` (`knowledge_chunks`):** Authoritative engineering chunk entity established in M7.2/M7.3 with `id`, `tenantId`, `sourceFile`, `sourceSheet`, `sourceRow`, `sourcePage`, `projectNumber`, `authorityStatus`, `contentHash`, `chunkText`.
- **`KnowledgeArticle` (`knowledge_articles`):** Governed knowledge entity with M8.2 lifecycle state machine (`DRAFT` $\rightarrow$ `UNDER_REVIEW` $\rightarrow$ `PUBLISHED`), M8.3 immutable versioning/atomic supersession, and M8.4 decision drafting.
- **`KnowledgeSearchService` (`src/modules/knowledge/services/knowledge-search.service.ts`):** Unified multi-domain catalog search engine across articles, engineering documents, and catalog items.

## 2. Invariant & Boundary Verification

1. **G13 Read-Only Invariant:** G13 retrieval algorithms, BM25/Vector indexing, hybrid fusion, Phi-3 grounding, and the physical vault (`D:\Mitra3.0\MitraEngineeringLibrary`) remain 100% FROZEN and read-only.
2. **Evidence Immutability in Published Articles:** Evidence links can only be attached, modified, or detached while the article is in `DRAFT` state. In `PUBLISHED`, `SUPERSEDED`, `EXPIRED`, and `ARCHIVED` states, evidence links are immutable.
3. **Mandatory Evidence Validation:** Every chunk attached to an article must physically exist in `knowledge_chunks` for that exact `tenantId`. Arbitrary/spoofed user citations are rejected.
4. **Default Search Filtering:** Default Knowledge Article search must return strictly `status = 'PUBLISHED'`, `is_latest = true`, non-deleted, and tenant-scoped records. Draft, under review, rejected, expired, and superseded articles are excluded from public search by default.
5. **Deduplication & Idempotency:** The same chunk cannot be attached twice to the same article; attaching an existing chunk is deduplicated or idempotently handled.
