# M9.3 — Bottleneck Identification Specification
## Shop-Floor Work-Center Discovery & Risk Tier Hierarchy
**Milestone:** `M9.3`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Bottleneck Risk Tiers

- `CRITICAL`: $\text{predictedUtilizationRatio} \ge 1.30$ OR $P(\text{overload}) \ge 0.85$
- `HIGH`: $\text{predictedUtilizationRatio} \ge 1.05$ OR $P(\text{overload}) \ge 0.65$
- `MEDIUM`: $\text{predictedUtilizationRatio} \ge 0.85$ OR $P(\text{overload}) \ge 0.40$
- `LOW`: Otherwise ($\text{predictedUtilizationRatio} < 0.85$)

---

## 2. Ranking Algorithm

Scans all active machines for the tenant, evaluates forecast deficit and overload probability, and sorts:
1. `CRITICAL` risk tier
2. `HIGH` risk tier
3. Highest `predictedDeficitHours` descending
