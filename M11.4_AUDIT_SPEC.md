# M11.4 Audit Specification

## 1. Emitted Audit Events
- `ENGINEERING_REVIEW_OPENED`: Logged when an engineer opens a drawing review workspace.
- `ENGINEERING_FINDING_VIEWED`: Logged upon inspecting detailed DFM finding thresholds.
- `ENGINEERING_REASONING_VIEWED`: Logged when the 9-step reasoning chain is reviewed.
- `ENGINEERING_RECOMMENDATION_REVIEWED`: Logged during recommendation inspection.
- `ENGINEERING_RECOMMENDATION_ACCEPTED`: Emitted upon decision `ACCEPTED` with mandatory notes.
- `ENGINEERING_RECOMMENDATION_MODIFIED`: Emitted upon decision `MODIFIED` with revised parameters.
- `ENGINEERING_RECOMMENDATION_REJECTED`: Emitted upon decision `REJECTED` with rationale.
- `ENGINEERING_REVIEW_CANCELLED`: Emitted when an analysis review is deferred or cancelled.
