# M10.1 — Temporal & Provenance Specification
## Provenance Tiers, Historical Lineage & Temporal Invariants
**Milestone:** `M10.1`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Provenance Hierarchy

1. **`TRANSACTIONAL_EVENT` (Confidence: 1.0000):** Authoritative lineage emitted directly from bounded-context transactions (e.g., Drawing checked in, Work order created).
2. **`EXPLICIT_HUMAN` (Confidence: 1.0000):** Explicit relationship created or confirmed by an authorized engineer or planner.
3. **`IMPORTED_DATA` (Confidence: 0.9000):** Lineage extracted from legacy data migration or external ERP/MES imports.
4. **`AI_INFERRED` (Confidence: Variable $\in (0.0, 1.0)$):** Probabilistic or similarity-derived relationship (e.g. Cited Phi-3 RAG citation match).

---

## 2. Temporal Semantics

- Every node and edge maintains `valid_from`, `valid_to`, and `is_superseded`.
- When an entity or relationship is revised (e.g. Drawing Rev A $\to$ Rev B), the previous edge is marked `is_superseded = true` with `valid_to = NOW()`, preserving full auditability of "what was true at the time".
