# M9.5 — Risk Classification Specification
## Deterministic Planning Risk Tiers & Decision Rules
**Milestone:** `M9.5`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Risk Tier Matrix

| Risk Tier | Condition | Recommended Planner Action |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **`CRITICAL`** | $\hat{y}_{\text{delay}} > 10\text{ days} \land P(\text{delay}) > 0.75$ OR Overload $> 85\%$ with Deficit $> 20\text{h}$ | Immediate executive leveling & capacity escalation required |
| **`HIGH`** | $\hat{y}_{\text{delay}} > 5\text{ days} \lor P(\text{delay}) > 0.60$ OR Overload $> 70\%$ | Planner review and milestone buffer adjustment recommended |
| **`MEDIUM`** | $\hat{y}_{\text{delay}} > 2\text{ days} \lor P(\text{delay}) > 0.30$ OR Deficit $> 5\text{h}$ | Monitor active critical path milestones |
| **`LOW`** | $\hat{y}_{\text{delay}} \le 2\text{ days} \land P(\text{delay}) \le 0.30$ | Nominal execution within baseline tolerance |
