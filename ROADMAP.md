# MITRA Roadmap

> This roadmap describes the implementation journey toward the vision defined in PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md.

---

## Guiding Principle

Every implementation phase must move MITRA closer to becoming an Engineering Knowledge Operating System.

The roadmap may evolve. The Constitution should not.

---

## Phase 1 — Platform Foundation ✅

**Objective:** Build the technical foundation.

**Deliverables:**
- React Frontend
- NestJS Backend
- PostgreSQL
- Redis
- MinIO
- Authentication
- RBAC
- Audit Logging
- Docker Deployment
- Local AI Runtime
- Documentation Framework
- Data Library

**Status:** Completed

---

## Phase 2 — Commercial Platform

**Objective:** Digitize customer acquisition.

**Modules:**
- CRM
- Customer Management
- Contacts
- RFQ Management
- Quotation Management

**Outputs:** Customer opportunity becomes an engineering project.

---

## Phase 3 — Project Management

**Objective:** Manage complete mold development.

**Modules:**
- Projects
- Milestones
- Teams
- Tasks
- Schedules
- Deliverables

**Outputs:** Single source of truth for every mold project.

---

## Phase 4 — Engineering

**Objective:** Manage engineering activities.

**Modules:**
- Design Management
- Drawing Revision
- Design Traceability
- BOM
- Process Planning

**Outputs:** Complete engineering traceability.

---

## Phase 5 — Manufacturing

**Objective:** Digitize manufacturing execution.

**Modules:**
- Machine Planning
- Job Scheduling
- Work Orders
- Production Tracking
- Trial Management

**Outputs:** Real-time manufacturing visibility.

---

## Phase 6 — Quality

**Objective:** Ensure quality throughout lifecycle.

**Modules:**
- Inspection
- NCR
- CAPA
- Quality Reports

**Outputs:** Closed-loop quality management.

---

## Phase 7 — Service

**Objective:** Support molds after delivery.

**Modules:**
- Dispatch
- Installation
- Service History
- Spare Parts
- Warranty

**Outputs:** Complete lifecycle management.

---

## Phase 8 — Business Intelligence

**Objective:** Provide operational insights.

**Modules:**
- Dashboards
- KPIs
- Cost Analysis
- Delivery Analysis
- Production Analytics

**Outputs:** Decision support.

---

## Phase 9 — Engineering Intelligence

**Objective:** Transform MITRA into an Engineering Knowledge Operating System.

**Modules:**
- Engineering Knowledge Base
- Local AI Copilot
- Knowledge Graph
- Predictive Analytics

**Outputs:** Knowledge grows continuously from completed projects.

---

## Continuous Activities

These continue throughout every phase:
- Security
- Performance
- Documentation
- Testing
- Backup
- Monitoring
- UX Improvements
- AI Improvements

---

## Success Criteria

- MITRA enables the complete mold lifecycle while preserving engineering knowledge and maintaining customer ownership of data.
- Every completed project increases the intelligence of the platform.
