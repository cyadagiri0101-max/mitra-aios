## 1. UI Information Architecture

- **Machine Status Cards:** Real-time RPM, temperature, spindle load, and cycle-time deviation badge.
- **Active Operational Recommendations:** List of open `REVIEW` proposals with reasoning and evidence metrics.
- **Human Governance Modal:** Interactive modal with `Accept`, `Modify`, `Reject` actions and mandatory decision notes.
