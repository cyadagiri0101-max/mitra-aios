# M11.3 Engineering Reasoning Architecture Specification

## 1. Architectural Mission
M11.3 establishes an advisory multi-step engineering reasoning and cost synthesis engine that correlates geometric features, DFM findings, historical quality defects, material rheology, and manufacturing process plans into structured, explainable engineering assessments.

```text
Current Geometry + DFM Findings + Historical NCR Evidence + Manufacturing Context
                               ↓
                 9-Step Explicit Reasoning Chain
                               ↓
                 Evidence-Grounded Impact Synthesis
                               ↓
                  Governed 6-Category Cost Synthesis
                               ↓
                  Explainable Advisory Recommendation
                               ↓
                  Mandatory Human Engineering Review
```

## 2. Advisory Scope Boundary
- **Advisory Only**: M11.3 outputs recommendations (`REVIEW_GEOMETRY`, `INCREASE_WALL_THICKNESS`, `INCREASE_DRAFT`, etc.).
- **Zero Autonomous Mutation**: Does not mutate CAD files, 2D drawings, BOM revisions, routings, work orders, capacity schedules, or machine parameters.
- **Human Authority**: Only a human engineer can transition a recommendation to `ACCEPTED` or `MODIFIED` with mandatory decision notes.
