# M11.7 Human Governance & Zero Autonomous Execution Certification

## 1. Advisory Governance Architecture
- **Inviolable Principle**: AI suggests advisory proposals; human engineer reviews, modifies, and decides.
- **Zero Autonomous Execution**: The system cannot autonomously alter CAD geometries, 2D drawings, BOMs, routings, machine assignments, schedules, or work orders.
- **Mandatory Decision Notes**: Technical justification notes are mandatory before persisting any human review decision (`ACCEPTED`, `MODIFIED`, `REJECTED`, `CANCELLED`).
- **Audit Immutability**: All decisions record reviewer identity, timestamp, decision state, and SHA-256 evidence hash.
