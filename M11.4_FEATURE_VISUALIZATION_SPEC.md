# M11.4 Feature Visualization Specification

## 1. Supported Canonical Features
- `WALL_THICKNESS`: Cavity & core nominal wall sections with thickness measurements in `mm`.
- `DRAFT_ANGLE`: Side-wall release draft angles in `deg`.
- `RIB`: Structural stiffener ribs with root-to-wall thickness ratios.
- `BOSS`: Fastener mounting bosses with nominal outer/inner diameters.
- `HOLE`: Slender core pins and blind holes with depth-to-diameter $L/D$ ratios.
- `FILLET_RADIUS`: Internal and external fillet radii with stress concentration ratings.
- `UNDERCUT`: Lateral core slide undercuts and negative draft conditions.

## 2. Selection & Inspection Contract
When a feature is selected:
1. Emissive cyan highlight `#64FFDA` applied to 3D submesh.
2. DFM finding list automatically filters/highlights associated rule finding.
3. Feature measurement and threshold delta rendered in status panel.
