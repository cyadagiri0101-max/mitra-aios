## 1. Strategic Rationale

With EKOS now release-certified in `v5.0.0`, MITRA possesses a rich, multi-hop canonical knowledge graph connecting customer drawings, BOM revisions, shop-floor machine telemetry, mold trial observations, and quality NCRs.

The most transformative next step for MITRA is **Engineering Copilot 2.0**:

1. **DFM (Design for Manufacturing) Analysis:**
   - Multi-step automated reasoning on wall thickness, draft angles, undercut risks, parting line complexity, and gate placement.
2. **Telemetry-Grounded Mold Design Review:**
   - Correlating past mold trial defects (gate blush, flash, short shots) directly to CAD geometric features to prevent repeating tooling errors.
3. **Automated Quoting & Cost Breakdown Synthesis:**
   - Transforming RFQ drawings and CAD metadata into itemized mold manufacturing time and tooling cost estimates with full cited provenance.
4. **Governed Human Approval:**
   - All DFM reviews and cost recommendations are strictly advisory and require engineer sign-off.
