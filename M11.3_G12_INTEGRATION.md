# M11.3 G12 Knowledge Governance Integration

## 1. Zero Automatic Publication Policy
AI-generated engineering reasoning proposals must NEVER be automatically ingested or published into the G12 Knowledge Catalog as ground truth.

## 2. Knowledge Candidate Promotion Path
1. System produces `EngineeringReasoningResult`.
2. Qualified engineer reviews and marks `ACCEPTED` or `MODIFIED` with explanatory decision notes.
3. Outcome is validated on shop floor / trial production.
4. Human engineer explicitly nominates the verified outcome as a G12 Knowledge Candidate.
