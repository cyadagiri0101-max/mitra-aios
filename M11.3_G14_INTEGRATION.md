# M11.3 G14 Predictive Context Integration

## 1. Contextual Signal Exposure
M11.3 exposes derived engineering reasoning signals to G14 predictive models:
- DFM geometry risk index
- Historical defect recurrence probability
- Tooling complexity score
- Estimated cycle time penalty
- Estimated rework and cost impact

## 2. Immutable Model Weights Policy
- M11.3 reasoning signals do NOT automatically retrain or mutate G14 model weights.
- M11.3 does NOT automatically modify plant capacity, dispatch schedules, or machine queues.
