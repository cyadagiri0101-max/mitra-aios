## 1. Timeline Leveling Audit Events

- `G14_LEVELING_RECOMMENDATION_GENERATED`: Emitted when advisory recommendation is created in `GENERATED` state.
- `G14_LEVELING_RECOMMENDATION_REVIEWED`: Emitted when proposal moves to `UNDER_REVIEW`.
- `G14_LEVELING_RECOMMENDATION_ACCEPTED`: Emitted upon planner acceptance.
- `G14_LEVELING_RECOMMENDATION_MODIFIED`: Emitted when planner alters proposed parameters.
- `G14_LEVELING_RECOMMENDATION_REJECTED`: Emitted when proposal is declined with reason.
- `G14_LEVELING_RECOMMENDATION_APPLIED`: Emitted upon explicit execution of timeline change.
- `G14_LEVELING_RECOMMENDATION_CANCELLED`: Emitted upon proposal cancellation.
- `G14_LEVELING_RECOMMENDATION_EXPIRED`: Emitted when an unapplied proposal passes its expiry timestamp.
