# M11.3 Engineering Impact Specification

## 1. Impact Categories
The reasoning engine maps geometric defects and historical evidence into 6 core engineering impact categories:
1. `manufacturability`: Cavity fill dynamics, freeze-off, gate blush, weld line integrity.
2. `tooling_complexity`: Core pin deflection, EDM electrode requirements, slider/lifter actions.
3. `cycle_time`: Thermal cooling delays, holding pressure duration overhead.
4. `quality_risk`: First article dimensional non-conformance (FADIR) and cosmetic sink marks.
5. `rework_risk`: Secondary bench fitting, deburring, and mold modification risk.
6. `schedule_impact`: Trial delays and pilot iteration lead times.

## 2. Certainty Governance
Impacts must declare explicit certainty levels:
- `OBSERVED`: Grounded in verified shop floor NCR or physical trial observation.
- `LIKELY`: Supported by high topological similarity ($>0.85$) and deterministic physics.
- `POSSIBLE`: Theoretical failure mode without direct shop floor precedent.
- `UNKNOWN`: Insufficient evidence to establish risk level.
