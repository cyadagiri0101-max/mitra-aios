## 1. Predictive Provenance Architecture

- Every `G14LevelingRecommendation` connects via `PREDICTED_FROM` edges to its originating `PROJECT`, `MACHINE`, `MILESTONE`, and `G14_MODEL_REGISTRY` nodes.
- Provenance lookup exposes non-causal contributing signals, uncertainty intervals, and human approval boundaries without creating autonomous mutations.
