## 1. Authority Hierarchy

When assembling grounding context, chunks are sorted and filtered by document authority status:

$$\text{AUTHORITATIVE\_RELEASE} > \text{CURRENT\_WORKING} > \text{HISTORICAL\_REFERENCE} > \text{SUPERSEDED}$$

### Invariants:
1. **Authoritative Priority:** Production tooling releases are prioritized as the primary evidence.
2. **Current Working Inclusion:** In-progress engineering files (`CURRENT_WORKING`) are included when highly relevant.
3. **Superseded Exclusion:** Chunks marked `SUPERSEDED` are automatically excluded from the grounding prompt unless the user explicitly requests historical/superseded records or names the deprecated revision in the query.
4. **Relevance Invariant:** Authority status modulates priority but cannot promote completely irrelevant documents above exact identifier matches.
