**Milestone:** `M11.0`

## 1. Persona Readiness Assessment

### 1. Executive / Management Persona (Maturity: 90%)
- Portfolio delivery risk and machine bottlenecks visible via KPI cards and analytics.
- **Identified UX Gap:** Dedicated multi-plant executive comparative rollup view.

### 2. Production Planner Persona (Maturity: 92%)
- Timeline leveling recommendations explain capacity deficit and suggest governed machine/date shifts.
- Human review modal allows Accept/Modify/Reject with audit trail.

### 3. Tooling / Design Engineer Persona (Maturity: 88%)
- CAD metadata, drawing revisions, and BOM diffs available.
- **Identified UX Gap:** Direct CAD 3D canvas viewport embedding in EKOS graph inspector.

### 4. Quality & Audit Persona (Maturity: 94%)
- One-click ISO 9001 and AS9100 evidence package generation and export.
- Immediate SHA-256 integrity verification.

### 5. Service & Maintenance Persona (Maturity: 84%)
- Tool master history and failure tickets recorded.
- **Identified UX Gap:** Mobile-optimized field technician offline inspection mode.
