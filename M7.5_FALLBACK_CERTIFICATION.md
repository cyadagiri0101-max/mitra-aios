## 1. Deterministic Fallback Architecture

When the local Ollama LLM is offline, restarting, or timing out, MITRA does not fail with an unhandled exception or hallucinate. Instead, it activates the **Deterministic Grounded Synthesizer**:

$$\text{Synthesized Answer} = \text{"Based on the engineering knowledge library, the specification for } [P] \text{ is documented in } [REF-1]. \text{ Details: } [S] \text{ } [REF-1]."$$

Where:
- $P$ is the authentic `projectNumber` or entity title.
- $S$ is the verified first line snippet of the top-ranked retrieved chunk.
- `[REF-1]` is deterministically mapped to the top chunk in `CitationRegistry`.

## 3. Fallback Integrity Guarantees

1. **Zero Hallucination Guarantee:** Deterministic synthesis constructs answers using literal strings from verified retrieved chunks. It is incapable of generating out-of-context tokens.
2. **Provenance Preservation:** Full metadata (`chunkId`, `sourceFile`, `sourceSheet`, `sourceRow`) is attached to `citations[0]`.
3. **Multi-Tenant Safety:** The fallback consumes candidates strictly from the tenant-filtered retrieval pool.
