## 1. Feature Attribution & Signal Directions

1. **Contribution Formula:** $\text{contribution}_j = w_j \cdot x_j$
2. **Direction Classification:**
   - $\text{contribution}_j > 0.1$: `INCREASES_CAPACITY_PRESSURE`
   - $\text{contribution}_j < -0.1$: `DECREASES_CAPACITY_PRESSURE`
   - Otherwise: `NEUTRAL`
3. **Primary Contributors:** Top 5 ranked features by absolute contribution magnitude.
4. **Advisory Language:** Terminology strictly uses "contributing signal" rather than "cause" to preserve human decision authority.
