## 3. Security & Privacy Constraints

1. **Client Control:**
   - The fields `tenantId`, `actorId`, `occurredAt`, `eventType`, and `aggregateId` are strictly generated by the server.
   - The client has zero ability to forge or override event metadata.
2. **Data Minimization:**
   - Event payloads contain operational identifiers and aggregates.
   - They do NOT contain sensitive personal data, unescaped raw HTML, private tokens, or proprietary CAD geometry coordinates.
3. **Immutability:**
   - Event records once persisted to `domain_outbox` cannot be altered by client mutations.
