**Milestone:** `M11.1`

## 1. Versioning Protocol

- Every DFM finding records `ruleId` and `ruleVersion` (e.g. `DFM-WALL-001 v1.0`).
- If an engineering rule threshold is modified in future releases, historical findings preserve their evaluation rule version for compliance audits.
