## 1. Cryptographic Digest Calculation

- `artifactHash`: SHA-256 digest of `{ capability, modelVersion, featureVersion, weights, intercept, residualStdDev }`.
- `provenanceHash`: SHA-256 digest of training snapshot IDs and sample records.
- **Integrity Gate:** `activateModel()` dynamically re-computes `artifactHash` over the stored weights and intercept; if mismatch detected, emits `G14_MODEL_INTEGRITY_FAILURE` and aborts activation.
