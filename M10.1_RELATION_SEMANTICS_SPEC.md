# M10.1 — Relation Semantics Specification
## Directed Relationship Types & Cardinality Constraints
**Milestone:** `M10.1`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Semantic Relationship Matrix

| Relation Type | Source Entity Types | Target Entity Types | Semantics & Directionality |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **`DERIVED_FROM`** | `DRAWING`, `BOM`, `CAD_MODEL` | `CAD_MODEL`, `DRAWING` | Version lineage or transformation (DAG, cycles forbidden) |
| **`REFERENCES`** | `WORK_ORDER`, `INSPECTION_PLAN` | `DRAWING`, `PROCESS_PLAN` | Operational execution references engineering specification |
| **`FULFILLS`** | `WORK_ORDER`, `PROJECT` | `RFQ`, `SALES_ORDER`, `MILESTONE` | Manufacturing or project milestone fulfills commercial commitment |
| **`CONSTRAINS`** | `MACHINE`, `TOOL_MASTER` | `PROCESS_PLAN`, `WORK_ORDER` | Physical shop-floor tooling or machine capability bounds |
| **`OBSERVED_IN`** | `NCR`, `DEFECT_RECORD` | `TRIAL_OBSERVATION`, `WORK_ORDER` | Quality deviation discovered during trial or machining |
| **`RESOLVED_BY`** | `NCR`, `CAPA` | `ECR_ECO`, `PROCESS_PLAN_REVISION` | Root-cause resolution engineering change |
| **`CITED_BY`** | `KNOWLEDGE_ARTICLE` | `DRAWING`, `TRIAL_OBSERVATION`, `NCR` | Formal engineering knowledge article cites operational artifact |
| **`PREDICTED_FROM`**| `LEVELING_RECOMMENDATION` | `PROJECT`, `MACHINE` | G14 predictive model produces advisory leveling proposal |
