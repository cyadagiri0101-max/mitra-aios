# M11.6 Human Governance Certification Document

## 1. Human-in-the-Loop Decision Governance
- State Machine: `GENERATED` $\rightarrow$ `UNDER_REVIEW` $\rightarrow$ `ACCEPTED` / `MODIFIED` / `REJECTED` / `CANCELLED`.
- Mandatory technical decision notes enforced before any review status transition is persisted.
- Strict advisory boundary: AI suggests; human engineer decides.
- Zero autonomous modification of CAD geometry, drawings, BOMs, routings, or schedules.
