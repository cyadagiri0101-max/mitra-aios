## 1. Data Quality Transformations

| Condition | Handling Strategy | Mitigation |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Missing Planned/Delivery Dates** | Fallback to `project.createdAt` or duration $= 1$ day | Prevents division by zero in ratios |
| **Negative Durations** | Clamp with $\max(0, \text{duration})$ | Eliminates clock skew anomalies |
| **Missing Baselines** | Flagged in `hasMissingBaselines: true` | Model can down-weight baseline features |
| **Zero Work Orders / Milestones** | Defaults ratio to $0.0$ | Prevents `NaN` in feature vectors |
| **Overloaded Capacity Hours** | Clamped to reasonable max ($200\%$) | Prevents extreme numerical instability |
