## 1. Citation Validation Engine Overview

The MITRA citation engine enforces mathematical determinism between text claims generated by AI models (or deterministic fallbacks) and verified source records in the Engineering Knowledge Library.

```mermaid
graph TD
    A["Raw Model Answer with [REF-x] Tags"] --> B["EngineeringCitationValidatorService"]
    B --> C{"Is [REF-x] in CitationRegistry?"}
    C -- "YES" --> D["Attach Full Chunk Provenance & Metadata"]
    C -- "NO (e.g. [REF-999])" --> E["Strip Phantom Citation Tag & Log Detection"]
    D --> F["Validated Response with Canonical Citation Payload"]
    E --> F
```
