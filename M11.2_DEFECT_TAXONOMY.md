# M11.2 — Defect Taxonomy Specification
## Authoritative Manufacturing & Tooling Defect Taxonomy
**Milestone:** `M11.2`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Supported Taxonomy Codes

- `SHORT_SHOT`: Incomplete cavity filling due to thin sections or freezing.
- `FLASH`: Excess material escaping parting line or shutoffs.
- `SINK_MARK`: Localized surface depression over thick ribs or bosses.
- `WARPAGE`: Differential cooling and non-uniform shrinkage causing distortion.
- `BURNT_MARK`: Trapped air/gas combustion in blind pockets.
- `EJECTION_MARK`: Pin drag marks caused by insufficient draft or high ejection force.
- `CORE_PIN_DEFLECTION`: Bending of slender core pins under injection melt pressure.
- `TOOL_BREAKAGE`: Mechanical fracture of mold cores, slides, or lifters.
- `DIMENSIONAL_VARIATION`: Out-of-tolerance dimensions post-cooling.
- `COOLING_VARIANCE`: Non-uniform cooling channel temperature distribution.
- `CYCLE_TIME_VARIANCE`: Excessive cooling requirements due to heavy wall thickness.
- `SURFACE_DEFECT`: Scuffing, flow lines, or weld line cosmetics.
- `CRACK`: Stress cracking at sharp internal corners.
- `THIN_WALL_FAILURE`: Premature structural rupture during dynamic loads.
- `OTHER`: Unclassified historical quality findings.
