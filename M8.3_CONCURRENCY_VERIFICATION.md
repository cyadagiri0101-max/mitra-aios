## 1. Concurrency Scenarios Evaluated

### Scenario A: Duplicate Concurrent Publish on Single Revision (v2)
- **Condition:** Two quality reviewers simultaneously click "Approve & Publish" on the same revision `v2`.
- **Expected Behavior:** Exactly one request enters the critical section and commits `v2` as `PUBLISHED`. The second concurrent request reads the updated status (`PUBLISHED` or locked) and is safely rejected with `BadRequestException('Invalid state transition: Cannot transition KnowledgeArticle from PUBLISHED to PUBLISHED')`.
- **Outcome:** Zero duplicate supersessions; exactly one `is_latest = true`.

### Scenario B: Competing Revisions from Same Baseline (v2a and v2b)
- **Condition:** Two engineers create draft revisions `v2a` and `v2b` from `v1`.
- **Workflow:** 
  1. `v2a` is reviewed and published first. `v1` transitions to `SUPERSEDED` (`supersededById = v2a.id`).
  2. Later, when `v2b` attempts publication, the atomic supersession check detects that `v1` is no longer `PUBLISHED` (it is already `SUPERSEDED` by `v2a`).
  3. The service rejects publication of `v2b` with `BadRequestException('Predecessor article [v1] is already SUPERSEDED by [v2a]. Revision v2b must be re-based on the latest published article.')`.
- **Outcome:** Split-brain lineages are prohibited; the lineage remains strictly linear.
