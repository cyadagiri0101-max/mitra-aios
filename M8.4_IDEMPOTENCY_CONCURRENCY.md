# M8.4 — Idempotency & Concurrency Specification
## Duplicate Prevention, Retries & Revision Compatibility
**Milestone:** `M8.4`  
**Status:** **SPECIFICATION COMPLETE**

---

## 1. Idempotency & Duplicate Prevention Strategy

### Invariant: Exactly One Root Knowledge Article per Decision
An `EngineeringDecision` represents a single historical choice. Therefore, only **one active root KnowledgeArticle** (`parentArticleId = null`) may be generated from that decision within a tenant.

### Execution Flow:
1. When `createArticleDraftFromDecision(decisionId, userId, tenantId)` is called:
2. The service queries `knowledge_articles` for an existing root article where `decision_id = decisionId`, `parent_article_id IS NULL`, `tenant_id = tenantId`, and `deleted_at IS NULL`.
3. **If found:** The service idempotently returns the existing root article (HTTP 200/201) without inserting a duplicate record.
4. **If not found:** The service constructs and saves the new draft article and emits an audit event.

---

## 2. Interaction with M8.3 Revision Workflow

- Once a root `KnowledgeArticle` (`v1`) is generated and published, an engineer may call `createRevision()` to produce `v2` (`parentArticleId = v1.id`, `decisionId = decision.id`).
- When a draft-from-decision request is executed subsequently, the root check inspects `parent_article_id IS NULL`. It correctly returns `v1` (or the root baseline) and does NOT create a rogue `v3` or duplicate root.
- Revision lineages remain completely intact and undisturbed.

---

## 3. Concurrency Protection

- If two client requests arrive simultaneously:
  - Both queries check for the existing root article.
  - The unique slug and tenant-scoped query ensure that only one insertion occurs; subsequent retries receive the committed root record.
