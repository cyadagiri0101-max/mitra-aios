# M8.5 — Evidence Revision & Immutability Policy
## Baseline Cloning, Lifecycle Guards & Audit Traceability
**Milestone:** `M8.5`  
**Status:** **SPECIFICATION COMPLETE**

---

## 1. Revision Evidence Cloning Policy

When an engineer creates a child revision (`createRevision`):
$$\mathbf{Article\ v1\ (PUBLISHED)} \xrightarrow{\text{createRevision}} \mathbf{Article\ v2\ (DRAFT)}$$

1. **Baseline Evidence Inheritance:** All active `KnowledgeArticleEvidence` records attached to `v1` are cloned into new evidence records linked to `v2`.
2. **Independent Modification in Draft:** While `v2` is in `DRAFT`, engineers can attach new evidence or detach outdated evidence.
3. **Immutable Published Baseline:** The evidence attached to `v1` remains 100% immutable and unchanged, preserving historical sign-off integrity for ISO 9001 audits.

## 2. Article Lifecycle Evidence Permission Matrix

| Article Status | Can Attach Evidence? | Can Detach Evidence? | Can Re-order Evidence? |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **`DRAFT`** | **YES** | **YES** | **YES** |
| **`UNDER_REVIEW`** | **NO** (Locked for Review) | **NO** | **NO** |
| **`REJECTED`** | **NO** (Must reopen to DRAFT) | **NO** | **NO** |
| **`PUBLISHED`** | **NO** (Immutable Baseline) | **NO** | **NO** |
| **`SUPERSEDED`** | **NO** (Historical Archive) | **NO** | **NO** |
| **`EXPIRED`** | **NO** (Expired Archive) | **NO** | **NO** |
| **`ARCHIVED`** | **NO** (Retired) | **NO** | **NO** |
