## 1. Mathematical Formulation

1. **Closed-Form Ridge Regression:**
   $$\theta^* = (\tilde{X}^T \tilde{X} + \alpha I)^{-1} \tilde{X}^T y$$
   with 15 feature dimensions and Tikhonov regularizer $\alpha = 1.0$. Solved via Gaussian elimination with partial pivoting in `G14CapacityTrainingService`.
2. **Calibrated Overload Probability:**
   $$P(\text{overload}) = \frac{1}{1 + \exp\left(-\frac{\text{predictedUtilizationRatio} - 1.0}{0.15}\right)}$$
3. **Artifact Persistence:**
   Saved in `g14_capacity_model_artifacts` with weights, intercept, residual standard deviation $\sigma$, and SHA-256 provenance signature.
