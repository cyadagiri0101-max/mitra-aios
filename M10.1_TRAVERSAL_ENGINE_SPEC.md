# M10.1 — Graph Traversal Engine Specification
## Bidirectional BFS Lineage Traversal & Bounding Constraints
**Milestone:** `M10.1`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Traversal Modes

1. **`UPSTREAM` Lineage:** Follows incoming edges to determine the root origin and ancestral decisions leading to the target entity (e.g. Work Order $\to$ Drawing $\to$ CAD Model $\to$ RFQ).
2. **`DOWNSTREAM` Lineage:** Follows outgoing edges to identify all dependent operational entities (e.g. CAD Model $\to$ Drawings $\to$ Work Orders $\to$ Trials $\to$ Quality Records).
3. **`BIDIRECTIONAL` Lineage:** Traverses both incoming and outgoing edges concurrently up to `maxDepth` (bounded between 1 and 10).
