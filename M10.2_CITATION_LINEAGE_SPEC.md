## 1. Citation Lineage Mechanics

- Every cited chunk in G13 Cited RAG or G12 Knowledge Article references a canonical document coordinate in `MitraEngineeringLibrary`.
- The EKOS graph maps this citation as a `CITED_BY` edge with provenance type `TRANSACTIONAL_EVENT` (if derived from formal publication) or `AI_INFERRED` (if derived from probabilistic RAG embedding matching).
