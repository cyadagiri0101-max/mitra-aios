**Milestone:** `M10.2`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. REST Endpoints Ledger

- `GET /api/ekos/lineage/knowledge/:articleId`: Full upstream/downstream knowledge article lineage.
- `GET /api/ekos/lineage/entity/:entityType/:entityId/knowledge`: Associated knowledge articles for a domain entity.
- `GET /api/ekos/lineage/recommendation/:recommendationId/provenance`: G14 recommendation upstream evidence lineage.
- `POST /api/ekos/lineage/knowledge/link`: Explicit human linking between a knowledge article and a domain entity.
