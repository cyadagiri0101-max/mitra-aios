## 1. AS9100 Requirement Mapping

- **AS9100 8.1.4 (Prevention of Counterfeit Parts):** Tool master lineage and material certificates.
- **AS9100 8.3.5 (Design Outputs):** Complete drawing revision lineage with explicit provenance.
- **AS9100 8.5.2 (Identification & Traceability):** Full RFQ $\to$ Project $\to$ Drawing $\to$ Work Order $\to$ Machine $\to$ Inspection traceability.
- **AS9100 8.7 (Nonconformity Control):** Strict isolation and documentation of nonconforming parts and CAPA.
