## 1. Cross-Domain Intelligence Surfaces

- **G12 Knowledge Governance:** Converts recurring machine observations or trial anomalies into draft knowledge candidates for engineer review.
- **G13 Engineering Library:** Retrieves shop-floor telemetry evidence for cited RAG responses.
- **G14 Predictive Intelligence:** Supplies normalized cycle time and downtime telemetry as feature context.
