# M11.2 — G13 Citation Integration Specification
## Authoritative Evidence Grounding for Engineering QA
**Milestone:** `M11.2`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Citation Invariant

- G13 responses cite the underlying authoritative NCR or Trial record (e.g. `[NCR-SHORT_SHOT-HIST-01]`), never the derived correlation score itself.
- Extracted evidence context includes material, defect type, and root cause notes.
