# M11.5 Cache Governance Specification

## 1. Multi-Tenant & Revision-Partitioned Caching
- Cache keys for drawings, geometric features, and reasoning results are partitioned by `[tenantId, drawingId, drawingRevision]`.
- Cross-tenant cache poisoning is physically impossible as tenant isolation is enforced at key generation.
- Mutation invalidation: Review submissions invalidate active finding cache and update reasoning status.
