## 1. Upstream & Downstream Lineage

1. **Upstream Lineage:** Traces back from a `KNOWLEDGE_ARTICLE` node to its originating root cause entities (e.g. `NCR`, `TRIAL_OBSERVATION`, `DRAWING`).
2. **Supporting Evidence:** Pulls associated physical file citations (`KnowledgeArticleEvidence`) linked to specific page/row coordinates in `MitraEngineeringLibrary`.
3. **Downstream Usages:** Identifies active projects, process plans, or predictive leveling recommendations citing the knowledge article.
