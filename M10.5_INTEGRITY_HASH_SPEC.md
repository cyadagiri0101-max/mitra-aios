**Milestone:** `M10.5`

## 1. Hash Calculation Formula

Let $P$ be the canonical JSON serialization of `{ tenantId, projectId, framework, evidence, lineage }`:

$$\text{packageHash} = \text{SHA256}(P)$$

Integrity verification re-computes the hash and returns `isTamperFree: computedHash === storedHash`.
