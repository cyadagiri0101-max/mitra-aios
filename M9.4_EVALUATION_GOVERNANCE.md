# M9.4 — Evaluation Governance Specification
## Quality Gates, Evaluation Metrics & Activation Preconditions
**Milestone:** `M9.4`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Evaluation Gate Criteria

1. **Dataset Size:** Minimum 5 validated historical snapshots with observed ground truth targets.
2. **Metric Integrity:**
   - Regression Metrics: $\text{MAE} \ge 0$, $\text{RMSE} \ge 0$, $R^2 \in [0, 1]$.
   - Classification Metrics: $\text{Accuracy} \in [0, 1]$, $\text{BrierScore} \in [0, 1]$.
3. **Temporal Validation:** Chronological out-of-time evaluation must show non-negative improvement over naive baseline before approval.
