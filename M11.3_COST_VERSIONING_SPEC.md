# M11.3 Cost Versioning Specification

## 1. Immutability & Reproducibility
- Historical reasoning results store a snapshot of calculated costs, rates, and timestamps.
- Updating or deleting an `EngineeringCostConfiguration` does NOT retroactively alter historical `EngineeringReasoningResult` snapshots.
- Every cost component records `rateSource`, `rateTimestamp`, and `calculationMethod`.
