# M9.3 — Capacity Dataset Specification
## Historical Snapshot Storage, Ground Truth Targets & Minimum Thresholds
**Milestone:** `M9.3`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Storage & Schema

1. **Table:** `g14_capacity_snapshots`
2. **Scoping:** Strictly partitioned by `tenant_id` and `machine_id`.
3. **Supervised Target Fields:**
   - Continuous Target: `capacityDeficitHours` ($\max(0, \text{actualDemand} - \text{availableHours})$).
   - Binary Classification Target: `isOverloaded` ($\text{capacityDeficitHours} > 0$).
   - Utilization Target: `actualUtilizationRatio` ($\frac{\text{actualDemand}}{\text{availableHours}}$).
4. **Minimum Training Threshold:** Minimum 5 historical snapshots with non-null ground truth targets required for model fitting.
