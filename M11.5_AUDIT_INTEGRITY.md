# M11.5 Audit Integrity Specification

## 1. Audit Immutability
All human review actions (`ACCEPTED`, `MODIFIED`, `REJECTED`, `CANCELLED`) record immutable audit ledger entries containing:
- `actorId` & `actorEmail`
- `tenantId`
- `reviewedAt` timestamp (ISO 8601)
- `decisionStatus`
- `mandatoryDecisionNotes`
- `evidenceHash`
