# M10.0 — Candidate Capabilities Analysis
## Strategic Evaluation of Next Major Platform Capabilities
**Milestone:** `M10.0` (Discovery Gate)  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Candidate Capability Options

### Option A: Engineering Intelligence Expansion (Deep CAD / Drawing / Geometry RAG)
- **Scope:** Direct CAD feature extraction (STEP/IGES parsing), 3D mesh viewer in browser, similarity search across tool geometry, design rule automated checking against G13 knowledge library.
- **Pros:** Deepens the core engineering moat of MITRA; bridges CAD files directly to RAG.
- **Cons:** High computational overhead; client-side 3D rendering complexity; requires specialized CAD geometry parsers.
- **Prerequisites:** G13 Knowledge Library (Certified), M3 Engineering Kernel (Certified).

---

### Option B: Manufacturing Intelligence Closed-Loop (MES / IoT / Shop-Floor Execution)
- **Scope:** Connect project planning $\to$ work-centers $\to$ machine telemetry $\to$ live capacity feedback $\to$ trial observations $\to$ closed-loop quality analytics.
- **Pros:** Directly leverages M9.3 capacity forecasting and M9.5 leveling engine; creates real-time feedback loop from shop-floor to project timeline.
- **Cons:** Real shop-floor hardware interfaces require simulated telemetry feeds in test environments.
- **Prerequisites:** M4 MES/Quality (Certified), M9.3 Capacity Forecasting (Certified), M9.5 Leveling (Certified).

---

### Option C: Enterprise Management & Executive Business Intelligence (Profitability / Cost / KPI Rollups)
- **Scope:** Comprehensive management dashboards, multi-project cost rollups, planned vs actual margin tracking, delivery reliability scorecards, executive risk heatmaps.
- **Pros:** Immediate executive value; brings all financial and operational metrics together in unified management cockpits.
- **Cons:** Primarily aggregations and UI visualizations rather than core intelligence innovations.
- **Prerequisites:** M6 G11 BI (Certified), M9.2 Delay Predictions (Certified).

---

### Option D: Engineering Knowledge Operating System Consolidation (EKOS / Unified Graph)
- **Scope:** Unify G12 (Knowledge Articles), G13 (Engineering Library RAG), G14 (Predictive Intelligence), and Project Traceability into a single **Engineering Knowledge Operating System (EKOS)** platform layer.
- **Pros:** Fulfills the ultimate vision in `PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md` ("evolving into an engineering knowledge operating system"); harmonizes knowledge graph, vector search, predictive models, and project lifecycle into an integrated platform foundation.
- **Cons:** Broad architectural integration requiring rigorous multi-domain coordination.
- **Prerequisites:** G12 (Certified), G13 (Certified), G14 (Certified).
