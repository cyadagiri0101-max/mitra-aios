## Quality States & Extraction Handling
**Milestone:** `M11.1`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Quality Extraction States

- `VALID`: Successfully extracted and verified within physically plausible bounds.
- `PARTIAL`: Incomplete dimensional metadata extracted.
- `UNSUPPORTED`: Non-standard or unparseable CAD binary formats.
- `AMBIGUOUS`: Conflicting dimensional markers in drawing views.
- `INVALID`: Unparseable syntax or corrupted metadata.
- `QUARANTINED`: Out-of-physical-range dimensions (e.g. wall thickness $\le 0\text{ mm}$ or bounding box $> 5000\text{ mm}$).
