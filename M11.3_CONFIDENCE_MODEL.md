# M11.3 Multi-Factor Confidence Model Specification

## 1. Deterministic Confidence Formulation
M11.3 does not generate opaque single-number AI confidence guesses. Confidence is deterministically computed from 5 weighted factors and 2 explicit penalty adjustments:

$$\text{Base Score} = w_1 \cdot Q_{\text{evidence}} + w_2 \cdot R_{\text{feature}} + w_3 \cdot R_{\text{historical}} + w_4 \cdot C_{\text{context}} + w_5 \cdot A_{\text{source}}$$

Where:
- $w_1 = 0.25$ (Evidence Quality)
- $w_2 = 0.25$ (Feature Topological Relevance)
- $w_3 = 0.20$ (Historical Defect Record Strength)
- $w_4 = 0.15$ (Context Completeness / Verified Assumptions Ratio)
- $w_5 = 0.15$ (Source Authority)

$$\text{Final Confidence} = \text{Base Score} \times (1.0 - P_{\text{contradiction}}) \times (1.0 - P_{\text{unverified}})$$

Where:
- $P_{\text{contradiction}} = 0.25$ if conflict exists, else $0.0$
- $P_{\text{unverified}} = \sum \text{impactOnConfidence}$ for unverified assumptions.
