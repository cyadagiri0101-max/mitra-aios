**Milestone:** `M9.5`

---

## 1. Planner Review Sequence

1. **Discovery:** Planner opens project timeline view and observes G14 AI Risk Indicator and pending Leveling Recommendations.
2. **Analysis:** Planner inspects the predicted slip, uncertainty interval, and non-causal contributing signals.
3. **Decision Options:**
   - **Accept:** Agrees with advisory leveling proposal.
   - **Modify:** Adjusts buffer parameters or dates while preserving the original recommendation lineage.
   - **Reject:** Declines proposal with mandatory structured reason.
4. **Explicit Application:** Planner triggers explicit Apply dialog; system applies schedule mutation transactionally.
