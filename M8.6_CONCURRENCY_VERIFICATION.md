**Milestone:** `M8.6`

## 1. Concurrency Invariants

- **Single Latest Published Invariant:** Across any article family, exactly one article possesses `status = 'PUBLISHED'` and `is_latest = true`.
- **Competing Revisions Guard:** If two child revisions (`v2a` and `v2b`) originate from `v1`, publication of `v2a` supersedes `v1`. Subsequent publication of `v2b` detects `v1.status === SUPERSEDED` and rejects with `BadRequestException`, preventing split-brain lineages.
- **ACID Transaction:** Child publication and predecessor supersession execute within `DataSource.transaction`.
