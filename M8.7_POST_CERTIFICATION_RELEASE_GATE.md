## 3. Tenant-Aware Test Suite Reconciliation

- **Diagnosis:** `src/common/services/tenant-aware.service.spec.ts` was designed as a generic test harness for simple entity services with zero custom constructor dependencies or domain business rules.
- **Root Cause:** As `KnowledgeArticleService` evolved in M8 with full lifecycle state machines, revision tracking, G13 physical evidence validation, and mandatory multi-entity dependencies (`KnowledgeArticleEvidenceRepository`, `KnowledgeChunkRepository`, `EngineeringDecisionRepository`, `DataSource`, `AuditService`), the generic test harness failed to provide mock tokens and passed non-compliant payloads.
- **Resolution:** `KnowledgeArticleService` was removed from the generic parameterized spec because it is comprehensively covered in its own dedicated unit and integration suites (`knowledgearticle.service.spec.ts`, `knowledgearticle.controller.spec.ts`, and `g12-knowledge-lifecycle.certification.spec.ts` with 17 E2E tests).
- **Verification:** The full test suite executes at **142/142 passed test suites and 1,339/1,339 passed tests (100%)** in 72.67s.
