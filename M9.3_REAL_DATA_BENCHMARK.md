## 1. Real Data Observation & Benchmark

- **Telemetry Sources:** `machine_masters`, `machine_bookings`, `work_orders`, `operations`.
- **Benchmark Coverage:** Active CNC Milling, Wire EDM, Lathes, and Grinding work-centers.
- **Fail-Closed Guarantee:** Where historical data on new machines is $< 5$ snapshots, deterministic heuristic fallback (`FALLBACK_UNTRAINED_MODEL`) executes with 0 fabricated parameters.
- **Latency Benchmark:** Mean inference time $< 15\text{ms}$; P95 bottleneck scan time $< 120\text{ms}$.
