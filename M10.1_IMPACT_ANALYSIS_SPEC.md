## 1. Blast-Radius Scoring Model

The blast-radius impact analysis algorithm calculates a normalized impact risk score ($S_{\text{blast}} \in [0, 100]$):

$$S_{\text{blast}} = \min\left(100, N_{\text{impacted}} \times 15 + N_{\text{critical}} \times 20\right)$$

Where:
- $N_{\text{impacted}}$ is the total number of unique downstream entities reached.
- $N_{\text{critical}}$ is the number of active `WORK_ORDER`, `MILESTONE`, or `NCR` entities requiring operational re-validation.
