# M11.3 G13 Citation & Retrieval Integration

## 1. Grounded Citation Hierarchy
- Reasoning conclusions cite concrete evidence records (`sourceEntity`, `sourceId`, `sourceRevision`).
- Citations resolve strictly to authoritative underlying records (e.g. `dfm_findings`, `historical_defect_correlations`, `geometric_features`).
- AI reasoning steps are never cited as if they were raw historical facts.
