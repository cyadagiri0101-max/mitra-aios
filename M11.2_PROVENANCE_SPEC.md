## 1. Provenance Context Schema

- `tenantId`: Authoritative tenant ID.
- `sourceEntity`: Originating DFM Finding or Quality NCR.
- `sourceRevision`: Specific drawing revision.
- `correlationVersion`: Version of correlation algorithm (`1.0`).
- `evidenceReferences`: Explicit list of source `ncrIds`, `trialObservationIds`, and `capaIds`.
