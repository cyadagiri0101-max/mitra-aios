## 1. Projected Relationships

- Operator and mold trial observations are projected into `ekos_graph_nodes` with `entity_type = 'TRIAL_OBSERVATION'`.
- `OBSERVED_IN` edges link observation nodes to `MACHINE` and `WORK_ORDER` nodes with `TRANSACTIONAL_EVENT` provenance.
