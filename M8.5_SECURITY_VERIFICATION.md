## 2. Anti-Spoofing & Integrity Controls

1. **Physical Chunk Verification:** Every `chunkId` passed in `attachEvidence` must exist in `knowledge_chunks` with `tenantId === requestedTenant`.
2. **Immutable Published Articles:** Attempts to attach or detach evidence from `PUBLISHED`, `SUPERSEDED`, `EXPIRED`, `ARCHIVED`, or `UNDER_REVIEW` articles are rejected with `BadRequestException`.
3. **Transaction Rollback:** If 10 chunks are submitted and even 1 fails validation or belongs to another tenant, the entire operation is aborted with zero records inserted and zero false audit events.
