## 1. Multi-Dimensional Risk Synthesis

The timeline risk aggregation engine synthesizes predictive signals across three governed layers:

1. **Project Dimension (M9.2):**
   - Predicted project completion delay in days ($\hat{y}_{\text{delay}}$).
   - Calibrated delay probability ($P(\text{delay}) \in [0, 1]$).
   - Residual uncertainty intervals $[\text{lowerBound}, \text{upperBound}]$.
   - Feature attribution vectors identifying top contributing risk factors.

2. **Machine & Work-Center Dimension (M9.3):**
   - Work-center capacity utilization ratio.
   - Predicted deficit hours.
   - Machine overload probability.
   - Shop-floor bottleneck ranking tier.

3. **Model Governance Dimension (M9.4):**
   - Active champion model version (`G14_DELAY_RIDGE_V1`, `G14_CAPACITY_RIDGE_V1`).
   - Cryptographic artifact integrity verification.
