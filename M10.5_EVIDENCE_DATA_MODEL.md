## 1. Table Schema: `compliance_packages`

- `id`: UUID Primary Key
- `tenant_id`: UUID
- `project_id`: UUID
- `framework`: `ISO_9001` | `AS9100` | `IATF_16949`
- `scope`: `FULL_PROJECT_TRACEABILITY`
- `version`: Integer (sequential)
- `completeness_status`: `COMPLETE` | `PARTIAL` | `MISSING` | `INVALID` | `SUPERSEDED`
- `package_hash`: SHA-256 (64 hex characters)
- `generator_version`: `MITRA_EKOS_COMPLIANCE_v5.0`
- `evidence_manifest`: JSONB
- `lineage_manifest`: JSONB
- `audit_manifest`: JSONB
- `gaps_and_warnings`: JSONB
