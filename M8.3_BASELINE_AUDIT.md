## 1. Executive Summary

Milestones M8.1 and M8.2 established:
1. **Persistence Schema (`1700000000044-M8KnowledgeLifecycle.ts`):** `version`, `is_latest`, `parent_article_id`, `superseded_by_id`, `project_id`, `decision_id`, `rejection_reason`, `expires_at`.
2. **Lifecycle State Machine:** `DRAFT` $\rightarrow$ `UNDER_REVIEW` $\rightarrow$ `PUBLISHED` with review rejection (`REJECTED` $\rightarrow$ `DRAFT`), terminal states (`SUPERSEDED`, `EXPIRED`), and published article mutation protection.

M8.3 builds upon this foundation by implementing:
- **`createRevision` API & Service:** Creating child revisions (`v2`, `v3`) in `DRAFT` from `PUBLISHED` parent articles without in-place mutation of the published baseline.
- **Atomic Supersession Transaction:** When child revision `v2` is approved/published, `v2` transitions to `PUBLISHED` (`isLatest = true`) and `v1` transitions to `SUPERSEDED` (`isLatest = false`, `supersededById = v2.id`) within a single database transaction (`DataSource.transaction`).
- **Concurrency & Rollback Protection:** Ensuring zero split-brain states where two versions are active or an inconsistent latest flag exists.

## 3. Invariants to Enforce in M8.3

1. **Published Immutability:** `PATCH /articles/:id` remains blocked on `PUBLISHED` records. Modifying content requires `POST /articles/:id/revisions`.
2. **Exact Increment:** `child.version === parent.version + 1`.
3. **Traceability:** `child.parentArticleId === parent.id`, `parent.supersededById === child.id`.
4. **Single Latest Published:** Exactly one article in an active lineage has `status = PUBLISHED` and `isLatest = true`.
5. **Atomic Commit:** If an error occurs during revision publication, neither child nor parent transitions state.
