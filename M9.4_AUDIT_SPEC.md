**Milestone:** `M9.4`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Registry Audit Events

- `G14_MODEL_REGISTERED`: Model artifact registered in `TRAINED` state.
- `G14_MODEL_EVALUATED`: Model validation metrics verified and transitioned to `EVALUATED`.
- `G14_MODEL_APPROVAL_REQUESTED`: Formal sign-off workflow initiated (`PENDING_APPROVAL`).
- `G14_MODEL_APPROVED`: Authorized sign-off completed (`APPROVED`).
- `G14_MODEL_REJECTED`: Formal rejection during review (`REJECTED`).
- `G14_MODEL_ACTIVATED`: Model promoted to active champion (`ACTIVE`).
- `G14_MODEL_RETIRED`: Active model superseded and retired (`RETIRED`).
- `G14_MODEL_ROLLED_BACK`: Champion rolled back to previous model (`ROLLED_BACK`).
- `G14_MODEL_INTEGRITY_FAILURE`: Emitted upon cryptographic hash mismatch.
