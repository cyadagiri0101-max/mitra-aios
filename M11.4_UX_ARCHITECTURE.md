# M11.4 UX Architecture Document

## 1. Architectural Philosophy
The MITRA M11.4 Human-in-the-Loop DFM Review workspace is designed as a mission-critical engineering cockpit. It connects raw 3D/2D CAD geometry with deterministic rule evaluations, empirical quality records, 9-step reasoning transformations, governed cost models, and human approval workflows.

## 3. Interaction Design Principles
- **Spatial Grounding**: Selecting a finding focuses the 3D camera and highlights the feature in cyan/emissive glow.
- **Traceability at a Glance**: Every observation and recommendation links directly to an authoritative source record.
- **Fail-Safe Accessibility**: Graceful fallback to structured 2D tabular mode if WebGL fails.
