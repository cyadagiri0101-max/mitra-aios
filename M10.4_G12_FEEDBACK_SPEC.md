# M10.4 — G12 Knowledge Feedback Specification
## Feedback Routing from Operational Observations to Knowledge Articles
**Milestone:** `M10.4`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Feedback Routing Rules

- Recurring process deviations (e.g. repeated holding pressure drops or runner freeze anomalies) generate draft knowledge recommendations.
- Human review remains strictly mandatory before publishing into the G12 Knowledge Catalog.
