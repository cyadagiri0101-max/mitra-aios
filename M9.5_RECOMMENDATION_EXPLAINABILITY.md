# M9.5 — Recommendation Explainability Specification
## Non-Causal Attribution & Transparent Rationale
**Milestone:** `M9.5`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Explainability Principles

- **Non-Causal Language:** Telemetry signals are described as "contributing signals" or "correlated factors", never as deterministic "causes".
- **Transparent Attribution:** Every recommendation explicitly lists:
  - Top contributing features and their signal directions.
  - Baseline comparison ($\Delta\text{Risk}$).
  - Uncertainty intervals derived from out-of-sample residuals.
