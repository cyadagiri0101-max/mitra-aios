# M9.1 — Feature Contract Specification
## G14 Feature Vectors, Families & Dense Representations
**Milestone:** `M9.1`  
**Feature Version:** `G14_FEATURES_V1`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Structured Feature Families

1. **Project Features:**
   - `projectAgeDays`: Elapsed days since project creation.
   - `totalMilestonesCount`: Total milestones scheduled.
   - `completedMilestonesCount`: Milestones completed prior to cutoff.
   - `milestoneCompletionRatio`: $\frac{\text{completed}}{\text{total}}$.
   - `cumulativeScheduleVarianceDays`: Sum of milestone delay days relative to planned dates.
   - `overdueMilestonesCount`: Incomplete milestones past planned date.
   - `remainingPlannedDurationDays`: Days remaining until target delivery date.

2. **Execution Features:**
   - `totalWorkOrdersCount`: Total work orders released.
   - `completedWorkOrdersCount`: Work orders finished prior to cutoff.
   - `workOrderCompletionRatio`: $\frac{\text{completed WO}}{\text{total WO}}$.
   - `cumulativeWorkOrderDelayDays`: Sum of work order delay days.
   - `totalTrialsCount`: Mold trials executed.
   - `failedTrialsCount`: Trials resulting in `FAIL`.
   - `trialFailureRate`: $\frac{\text{failed trials}}{\text{total trials}}$.

3. **Capacity Features:**
   - `totalDemandHours`: Total planned engineering design load hours.
   - `availableCapacityHours`: Standard workstation/engineer hours.
   - `capacityGapHours`: $\max(0, \text{demand} - \text{capacity})$.
   - `averageUtilizationPct`: Design workstation utilization percentage.
   - `isOverloaded`: Boolean flag ($\text{demand} > \text{capacity}$).

4. **Quality Features:**
   - `totalNcrCount`: Total non-conformance records raised.
   - `openNcrCount`: Active/unresolved non-conformance records.
   - `criticalNcrCount`: NCRs with `CRITICAL` severity.
   - `capaCount`: NCRs requiring CAPA.
   - `reworkFrequency`: $\frac{\text{total NCR}}{\text{total WO}}$.

5. **Dependency & Temporal Features:**
   - `blockedMilestonesCount`: Milestones flagged as `DELAYED`.
   - `elapsedProjectDays`: Days elapsed since enquiry/start.
   - `projectDurationDays`: Total planned project window.
   - `timeElapsedRatio`: $\frac{\text{elapsed}}{\text{total duration}}$.

6. **Dense Representation:**
   - 20-dimensional normalized vector $[f_0, \dots, f_{19}]$ standardized for direct consumption by ML models (M9.2).
