# M11.3 Reasoning Step Specification

## The 9 Deterministic Reasoning Steps

1. **STEP 1: IDENTIFY_GEOMETRIC_FINDING**
   - Inputs: DFM Finding ID, Geometric Feature ID.
   - Transformation: Extract dimensional parameters, rule definitions, and deviation ratios.
   - Output: Rule identifier, observed vs expected threshold, severity.

2. **STEP 2: RETRIEVE_HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE**
   - Inputs: Finding ID, Drawing Revision, Material, Process.
   - Transformation: Correlate geometric pattern with shop floor NCRs, CAPAs, and trial observations.
   - Output: Matched records count, defect categories.

3. **STEP 3: EVALUATE_FEATURE_SIMILARITY**
   - Inputs: Finding topology, Feature measurements.
   - Transformation: Cosine topological similarity against validated engineering topologies.
   - Output: Similarity score (0.0 to 1.0).

4. **STEP 4: EVALUATE_MATERIAL_PROCESS_COMPATIBILITY**
   - Inputs: Material Profile, Process Routing Master.
   - Transformation: Rheological viscosity, shrinkage %, and thermal cooling gradient analysis.
   - Output: Material compatibility profile.

5. **STEP 5: EVALUATE_MANUFACTURING_IMPLICATIONS**
   - Inputs: Geometry, Tooling kinematics.
   - Transformation: Side-action mold complexity, core pin deflection risks, and cycle time penalties.
   - Output: Manufacturing overhead parameters.

6. **STEP 6: EVALUATE_HISTORICAL_DEFECT_RELEVANCE**
   - Inputs: Historical correlations, DFM rules.
   - Transformation: Contradiction filtering and root cause recurrence probability assessment.
   - Output: Contradiction state (`NO_CONFLICT`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, `CONFLICT_PRESENT`).

7. **STEP 7: CONSTRUCT_ENGINEERING_ASSESSMENT**
   - Inputs: Findings, Historical context, Kinematics.
   - Transformation: Cross-domain impact synthesis across manufacturability, tooling, cycle time, quality, rework, scrap.
   - Output: Structured engineering impacts with certainty levels (`OBSERVED`, `LIKELY`, `POSSIBLE`, `UNKNOWN`).

8. **STEP 8: ESTIMATE_OPERATIONAL_COST_IMPACT**
   - Inputs: Impact assessment, Authoritative cost configurations.
   - Transformation: 6-category cost calculation with 3-point uncertainty modeling (`low`, `expected`, `high`).
   - Output: Structured cost summary with unconfigured rate auditing.

9. **STEP 9: GENERATE_EXPLAINABLE_RECOMMENDATION**
   - Inputs: Assessment, Cost summary, Assumptions, Constraints.
   - Transformation: Advisory recommendation synthesis with rationale and audit lineage.
   - Output: Actionable recommendation object.
