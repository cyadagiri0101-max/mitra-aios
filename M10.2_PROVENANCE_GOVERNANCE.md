# M10.2 — Provenance Governance Specification
## Provenance Rules & Non-Equivalence of AI Inferences
**Milestone:** `M10.2`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Provenance Invariants

- **`TRANSACTIONAL_EVENT`:** Emitted directly from authoritative database mutations (e.g. Work Order created, Trial logged).
- **`EXPLICIT_HUMAN`:** Formally established by an authorized user (e.g. Engineer linking NCR to Knowledge Article).
- **`IMPORTED_DATA`:** Originating from external ERP/MES migrations.
- **`AI_INFERRED`:** Generated by machine learning or LLM citation match.
- **Strict Boundary:** `AI_INFERRED` edges are never treated as authoritative engineering facts without explicit human confirmation.
