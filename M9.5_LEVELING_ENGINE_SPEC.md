## 1. Recommendation Types

1. **`ADJUST_MILESTONE_DATE`:** Proposes shifting downstream milestone planned completion date to absorb predicted project slip while protecting customer commitments.
2. **`ESCALATE_CAPACITY`:** Proposes overtime authorization or workload offloading on work-centers with capacity deficit $> 0$.
3. **`SHIFT_OPERATION`:** Proposes re-sequencing non-critical operations.
4. **`REALLOCATE_MACHINE`:** Proposes shifting compatible jobs to available alternative machines.
5. **`SPLIT_BATCH`:** Proposes splitting large batch orders across parallel work-centers.
