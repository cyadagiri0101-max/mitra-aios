### Traceability Mapping:
- **REQ-G12-01 (Decision Formalization):** `EngineeringDecision` $\rightarrow$ `KnowledgeArticleService.createArticleDraftFromDecision` $\rightarrow$ `POST /api/knowledge/articles/from-decision/:id` $\rightarrow$ `knowledge_articles` $\rightarrow$ `g12-knowledge-lifecycle.certification.spec.ts` (Scenario A)
- **REQ-G12-02 (Evidence Grounding):** `KnowledgeArticleEvidence` $\rightarrow$ `KnowledgeArticleService.attachEvidence` $\rightarrow$ `POST /api/knowledge/articles/:id/evidence` $\rightarrow$ `knowledge_article_evidence` $\rightarrow$ `g12-knowledge-lifecycle.certification.spec.ts` (Scenario B & C)
- **REQ-G12-03 (Review & Rejection Lifecycle):** `ArticleStatus` $\rightarrow$ `KnowledgeArticleService.submitForReview` / `rejectReview` / `reopenRejected` $\rightarrow$ `POST /submit`, `/reject`, `/reopen` $\rightarrow$ `knowledge_articles.status` $\rightarrow$ `g12-knowledge-lifecycle.certification.spec.ts` (Scenario E & F)
- **REQ-G12-04 (Quality Publication):** `KnowledgeArticleService.approveAndPublish` $\rightarrow$ `POST /approve`, `/publish` $\rightarrow$ `knowledge_articles.status = PUBLISHED` $\rightarrow$ `g12-knowledge-lifecycle.certification.spec.ts` (Scenario G)
- **REQ-G12-05 (Governed Search Quality Gate):** `KnowledgeSearchService.search` $\rightarrow$ `POST /api/knowledge/search` $\rightarrow$ Filter `status = PUBLISHED AND is_latest = true` $\rightarrow$ `g12-knowledge-lifecycle.certification.spec.ts` (Scenario H & I)
- **REQ-G12-06 (Revisions & Evidence Inheritance):** `KnowledgeArticleService.createRevision` $\rightarrow$ `POST /:id/revisions` $\rightarrow$ `knowledge_articles.parent_article_id` $\rightarrow$ `g12-knowledge-lifecycle.certification.spec.ts` (Scenario J)

- **REQ-G12-08 (Historical Traceability):** `KnowledgeArticleService.getEvidence` $\rightarrow$ `GET /:id/evidence` $\rightarrow$ `knowledge_article_evidence` query $\rightarrow$ `g12-knowledge-lifecycle.certification.spec.ts` (Scenario N)
- **REQ-G12-09 (Expiry Governance):** `KnowledgeArticleService.markExpired` $\rightarrow$ `POST /:id/expire` $\rightarrow$ `knowledge_articles.status = EXPIRED` $\rightarrow$ `g12-knowledge-lifecycle.certification.spec.ts` (Scenario P)
- **REQ-G12-10 (Audit Traceability):** `AuditService.logBusinessEvent` $\rightarrow$ Event outbox/logger $\rightarrow$ `knowledge_articles` business events $\rightarrow$ `g12-knowledge-lifecycle.certification.spec.ts` (Scenario Q)
