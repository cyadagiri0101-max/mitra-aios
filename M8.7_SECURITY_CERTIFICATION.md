## 1. Security Invariants Attestation

1. **Multi-Tenant Scoping:** Every entity lookup, evidence attachment, revision lineage query, and search filter unconditionally scopes by `tenant_id`. Cross-tenant injection and IDOR attempts fail closed with 404/400.
2. **Role-Based Access Control:** `RolesGuard` strictly validates permissions across `ADMIN`, `MANAGEMENT`, `DESIGN`, `PLANNING`, `PRODUCTION`, `QUALITY`, and `SALES`.
3. **Published Record Immutability:** In-place modifications to published articles or their attached evidence are strictly rejected by service and state transition validators.
4. **Citation Anti-Spoofing:** Evidence references are physically verified against existing `knowledge_chunks` belonging to the calling tenant before linking.
5. **G13 Vault Protection:** The physical library `MitraEngineeringLibrary` remains 100% read-only with 0 modifications and 0 bytes drift.
