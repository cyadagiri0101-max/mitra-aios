## Chronological Dataset Construction, Target Ground Truth & Minimum Thresholds
**Milestone:** `M9.2`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Training Dataset Formation

1. **Source Pool:** Filtered from `g14_feature_snapshots` where `tenant_id = scopeTenant`, `feature_version = 'G14_FEATURES_V1'`, and `ground_truth_target IS NOT NULL`.
2. **Feature Matrix ($X \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times 20}$):** 20-dimensional normalized vector extracted strictly on or before `predictionCutoff`.
3. **Ground Truth Targets ($y \in \mathbb{R}^n$):**
   - Continuous Target: `projectDelayDays` (actual finish date vs target delivery date in days).
   - Binary Target: `isDelayed` ($\text{projectDelayDays} > 0$).
4. **Minimum Sample Threshold:** Minimum 5 validated historical snapshots required for training. If $n < 5$, training rejects with `BadRequestException`, and inference safely operates in `FALLBACK_UNTRAINED_MODEL` mode.
