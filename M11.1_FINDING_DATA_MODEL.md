# M11.1 — Finding Data Model Specification
## Schema & Lifecycle State Machine for DFM Findings
**Milestone:** `M11.1`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Finding Lifecycle

```
[OPEN] ──► [ACKNOWLEDGED] ──┬──► [ACCEPTED] ──► [RESOLVED]
                            ├──► [MODIFIED] ──► [RESOLVED]
                            └──► [REJECTED]
```
- Findings are strictly generated in `OPEN` status.
- Human engineering review transitions status and records `humanReviewerId`, `reviewedAt`, and `decisionNotes`.
