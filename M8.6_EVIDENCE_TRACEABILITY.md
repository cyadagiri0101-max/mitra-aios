**Milestone:** `M8.6`

## 1. Verified Evidence Invariants

1. **Physical Chunk Grounding:** Evidence links attached via `attachEvidence` reference verified G13 `KnowledgeChunk` records under tenant isolation.
2. **Deterministic Citation Labels:** Sequence numbers map monotonically to `[REF-1]`, `[REF-2]`, ... with source coordinates (e.g. `BM454 Partlist_RevA.xlsx#Inserts:R14`, `ISO_20482_Milling_Guidelines.pdf#page:5`).
3. **Immutability of Historical Evidence:** When `v1` is superseded by `v2`, `v1` historical evidence remains permanently intact and queryable for compliance audits.
4. **Baseline Evidence Inheritance:** Creating `v2` clones active evidence from `v1` into `v2` as the initial baseline, which can be modified in draft without affecting `v1`.
