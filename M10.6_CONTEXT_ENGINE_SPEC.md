# M10.6 — Context Engine Specification
## Cross-Domain Context Aggregation Engine
**Milestone:** `M10.6`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Engine Dimensions

- Commercial Commitment (RFQs, quotations, milestones)
- Engineering Artifacts (CAD models, drawing revisions, BOMs)
- Manufacturing Status (Active work orders, machines, operations)
- Quality Records (NCRs, CAPAs, trial observations)
- Governed Knowledge (Validated knowledge articles)
- Predictive Signals (Delay risk, capacity deficit risk, leveling recommendations)
