## Multi-Attribute Feature Similarity Formula
**Milestone:** `M11.2`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Mathematical Similarity Formula

$$\text{Similarity} = w_{\text{geom}} \cdot S_{\text{geom}} + w_{\text{mat}} \cdot S_{\text{mat}} + w_{\text{proc}} \cdot S_{\text{proc}}$$

Where:
- $w_{\text{geom}} = 0.50$, $w_{\text{mat}} = 0.30$, $w_{\text{proc}} = 0.20$
- $S_{\text{mat}} = 1.0$ (exact material match) or $0.7$ (same polymer family)
- $S_{\text{proc}} = 1.0$ (exact manufacturing process match)
