# M11.5 Database Performance Specification

## 1. Indexed Access Paths
- `geometric_features`: Indexed on `['tenant_id', 'drawing_id', 'drawing_revision']` and `['tenant_id', 'project_id']`.
- `dfm_findings`: Indexed on `['tenant_id', 'drawing_id', 'drawing_revision']` and `['tenant_id', 'severity']`.
- `historical_defect_correlations`: Indexed on `['tenant_id', 'drawing_id', 'drawing_revision']` and `['tenant_id', 'defect_type']`.
- `engineering_reasoning_results`: Indexed on `['tenant_id', 'finding_id']` and `['tenant_id', 'evidence_hash']`.
- `engineering_cost_configurations`: Indexed on `['tenant_id', 'rate_type']` and `['tenant_id', 'status']`.

## 2. Query Performance
All queries utilize index scans for tenant and revision scoping, avoiding full table sequential scans.
