# M8.6 — Failure Injection & Rollback Report
## Transaction Atomicity & False Audit Suppression
**Milestone:** `M8.6`  
**Status:** **CERTIFIED**

---

## 1. Failure Scenarios Tested

1. **Database Contention / Lock Error during Publication:**
   - Both child revision and parent predecessor remain in their exact prior states (`UNDER_REVIEW` and `PUBLISHED`).
   - Zero false audit events are emitted.
2. **Partial Batch Evidence Attachment Failure (4 valid, 1 invalid):**
   - The entire batch is aborted; zero evidence rows are saved.
   - Zero false audit events are emitted.
3. **Invalid Version Increment (Skipping Version Number):**
   - Transaction aborts with `BadRequestException`.
   - Predecessor remains `PUBLISHED`.
