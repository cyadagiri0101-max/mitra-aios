## 1. Executive Recommendation

The recommended strategic milestone is **M10 — Engineering Knowledge Operating System (EKOS) & Manufacturing Closed-Loop Platform Consolidation**.

### Core Rationale:
1. **Constitutional Alignment:** `PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md` explicitly defines MITRA's end-state as an **"Engineering Knowledge Operating System"**.
2. **Post-G14 Synergy:** Having completed G12 (Institutional Articles), G13 (Engineering Library RAG), and G14 (Predictive Intelligence & Leveling), the platform now possesses all four foundational intelligence engines.
3. **Harmonization & Closed-Loop Feedback:** M10 unifies these pillars with the operational manufacturing execution loop (MES $\leftrightarrow$ Capacity $\leftrightarrow$ Quality $\leftrightarrow$ Project Timeline $\leftrightarrow$ Knowledge Articles).

---

## 2. Key Capabilities of M10

1. **Unified EKOS Knowledge & Traceability Graph:** Visual cross-domain entity lineage linking Customer RFQ $\to$ CAD Drawings $\to$ Work Orders $\to$ Inspection NCRs $\to$ Service Logs $\to$ AI Knowledge Articles $\to$ Predictive Models.
2. **Manufacturing Closed-Loop Intelligence:** Real-time feedback from shop-floor machine telemetry and trial observations into G14 capacity forecasting and G12 knowledge capture.
3. **Enterprise Compliance & Audit Packager:** One-click automated ISO 9001 / AS9100 audit report generator compiling complete project-to-part traceability records.
