# M9.0 — Traceability Gap Analysis
## End-to-End Enterprise Lifecycle Traceability & Linkage Audit
**Milestone:** `M9.0`  
**Status:** **AUDITED**

---

## 1. Enterprise Thread Lifecycle Continuity

$$\text{RFQ} \xrightarrow{} \text{Quotation} \xrightarrow{} \text{Project} \xrightarrow{} \text{BOM/Drawing} \xrightarrow{} \text{Process Plan} \xrightarrow{} \text{Work Order} \xrightarrow{} \text{Trial} \xrightarrow{} \text{Inspection/NCR} \xrightarrow{} \text{Dispatch/Warranty} \xrightarrow{} \text{Decision/Knowledge}$$

### Verified Linkages:
- `projectId` propagates across Quotations, Drawings, BOMs, Work Orders, NCRs, Warranties, and Knowledge Articles.
- `decisionId` connects ECR/ECO engineering decisions to Knowledge Articles.
- `chunkId` connects Knowledge Articles to physical G13 engineering knowledge chunks.

### Identified Traceability Gaps:
1. **Predictive Traceability Gap:** Historical project variances and machine execution delays are recorded in database tables but lack a standardized feature-extraction pipeline to train and evaluate ML delay models.
2. **Graph Traversal Gap:** Tracing from a field warranty claim all the way back to raw CAD drawing chunk parameters requires multi-hop relational SQL queries rather than a unified Digital Thread lineage API.
