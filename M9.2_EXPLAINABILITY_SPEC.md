# M9.2 — Explainability Specification
## Feature Attribution, Contributing Signals & Risk Tier Classification
**Milestone:** `M9.2`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Explainability Protocol

1. **Contribution Scoring:**
   For each feature $j \in \{0, \dots, 19\}$, compute:
   $$\text{contribution}_j = w_j \cdot x_j$$
2. **Direction of Influence:**
   - $\text{contribution}_j > 0.1$: `INCREASES_DELAY`
   - $\text{contribution}_j < -0.1$: `DECREASES_DELAY`
   - Otherwise: `NEUTRAL`
3. **Primary Contributors:** Top 5 features sorted by $|\text{contribution}_j|$ descending.
4. **Risk Tiers:**
   - `CRITICAL`: $\hat{y} \ge 21 \text{ days}$ OR $P(\text{delay}) \ge 0.85$
   - `HIGH`: $\hat{y} \ge 10 \text{ days}$ OR $P(\text{delay}) \ge 0.65$
   - `MEDIUM`: $\hat{y} \ge 3 \text{ days}$ OR $P(\text{delay}) \ge 0.40$
   - `LOW`: Otherwise
5. **Non-Causal Governance:** Terminology strictly uses "contributing signal" rather than "cause" to prevent unsupported causal claims.
