## Revision Awareness, Temporal History & Supersession Invariants
**Milestone:** `M10.2`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Revision Invariants

1. When a new revision is introduced (e.g. Drawing `DWG-100` Rev A $\to$ Rev B), a distinct graph node is registered for `DWG-100@Rev B`.
2. Existing knowledge article links to `DWG-100@Rev A` remain historically intact.
3. If the knowledge article is updated to cite `Rev B`, the previous relationship edge is marked `is_superseded = true` and `valid_to = NOW()`, preserving the complete temporal audit trail.
