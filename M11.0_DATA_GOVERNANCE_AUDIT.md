# M11.0 — Data & Knowledge Governance Audit
## Provenance, Lineage, and Invariant Verification
**Milestone:** `M11.0`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Governance Evaluation

- **Authoritative Sources:** Each domain database (Projects, Drawings, Work Orders, NCRs) remains the single authoritative source of truth; EKOS serves strictly as a canonical lineage index.
- **DAG Cycle Prevention:** Cycles on `DERIVED_FROM` and `REVISES` relationships are strictly blocked in `EkosGraphService`.
- **AI Non-Authoritative Invariant:** All predictive leveling recommendations and AI-inferred edges are explicitly flagged with `isAiInferred: true` and cannot overwrite transactional records without human sign-off.
- **Audit Logging:** Every material generation, ingestion, query, and human review operation emits an immutable audit event to `AuditService`.
