## 2. Integration Design for M7.4

Rather than modifying certified M1–M6 AI modules, M7.4 implements a dedicated, isolated engineering grounding stack inside `src/modules/engineering-library/grounding`:
1. `EngineeringContextBuilderService`: Formats retrieved chunks into deterministic context blocks with unique `[REF-x]` tags and provenance metadata.
2. `EngineeringCitationValidatorService`: Enforces 100% citation validity and eliminates hallucinated references.
3. `EngineeringPhi3GroundingService`: Coordinates local Phi-3 prompting, structured parsing, confidence estimation, and fallback handling.
4. Exposes production endpoints `POST /api/ekl/ask` and `POST /api/ekl/ask/debug` in `EngineeringLibraryController`.
