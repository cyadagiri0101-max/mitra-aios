## 1. Executive Summary & Architecture

Milestone **M8.5** connects governed `KnowledgeArticle` records with certified G13 `KnowledgeChunk` retrieval evidence and enforces institutional quality gating on organizational knowledge search.

### Key Deliverables:
1. **Grounded Evidence Boundary:** `KnowledgeArticleEvidence` (`knowledge_article_evidence`) captures authoritative G13 snapshots: `chunkId`, `sourceFile`, `sourceSheet`, `sourceRow`, `sourcePage`, `sourceCoordinate`, `authorityStatus`, `contentHash`, and deterministic citations (`[REF-1]`, `[REF-2]`, ...).
2. **Evidence Validation & Anti-Spoofing:** Attached chunk IDs are physically verified against `knowledge_chunks` under the exact tenant scope. Cross-tenant or fabricated citations are rejected.
3. **Lifecycle Protection & Immutability:** Evidence links can only be attached, re-ordered, or detached while an article is in `DRAFT` state. Published, under review, superseded, and expired articles have 100% immutable evidence.
4. **Revision Evidence Inheritance:** Creating a child revision (`createRevision`) clones all verified baseline evidence to the new draft for continuous engineering traceability.
5. **Governed Search Quality Gate:** Default knowledge search strictly filters for `status = 'PUBLISHED' AND is_latest = true AND deleted_at IS NULL AND tenant_id = currentTenant`, excluding drafts, reviews, rejected drafts, and superseded versions from public discovery.
