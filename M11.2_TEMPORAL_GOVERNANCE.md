# M11.2 — Temporal Governance Specification
## Historical Lineage & Revision Integrity
**Milestone:** `M11.2`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Revision Invariants

- Historical NCRs, CAPAs, and trial observations remain strictly immutable.
- A new drawing revision (e.g. `Rev B`) creates separate defect correlation records without modifying or deleting `Rev A` correlation lineage.
- Historical defect trends remain auditable across the full lifecycle.
