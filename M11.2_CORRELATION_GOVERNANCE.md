# M11.2 — Correlation Governance Specification
## Correlation vs Causation & Human Sign-off Rules
**Milestone:** `M11.2`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Core Invariants

- **Correlation $\neq$ Causation:** Defect associations are statistical and historical context, not proof of causality.
- Only engineering records with confirmed root cause analysis by a human engineer can receive the `CONFIRMED_ENGINEERING_RELATIONSHIP` designation.
- Zero autonomous changes to tooling design, CAD models, BOMs, or machine setups.
