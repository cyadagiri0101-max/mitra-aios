## 1. Export Structure

- Manifest summary: `evidenceCount`, `lineageEdgeCount`, `auditEventCount`, `gapsCount`.
- Full serialized `evidenceItems` list with provenance.
- Full serialized `lineageEdges` list.
- Verification metadata: `packageHash`, `generatorVersion`, `generatedAt`.
