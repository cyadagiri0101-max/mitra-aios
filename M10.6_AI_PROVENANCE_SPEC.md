## 1. Provenance Schema

Every AI-derived answer returned by `/api/ekos/context/question` must return:

- `isAiInferred: true`
- `modelProvenance`: `{ model, temperature, groundedContextTokens }`
- `citedEntities`: Array of cited graph nodes with confidence scores.
