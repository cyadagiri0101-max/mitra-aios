## General guidance

- Preserve project prefixes and numeric codes.
- Use descriptive document type segments such as `Index Sheet`, `Process planning sheet`, `Partlist`, or `Cost-summary`.
- Preserve revision tokens like `RevA`, `RevB`, or `Rev` where present.
