**Milestone:** `M10.3`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Quality Tiers

- `VALID`: Conforms to unit, range, timestamp, and machine constraints.
- `OUT_OF_RANGE`: Exceeds physical sensor bounds; quarantined.
- `LATE`: Arrives $> 7\text{ days}$ post event-timestamp; recorded without altering historical state.
- `DUPLICATE`: Identical `(sourceId, signalType, eventTimestamp)`; handled idempotently.
