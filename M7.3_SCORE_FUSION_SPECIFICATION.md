# M7.3 — Score Fusion & Normalization Specification
## Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) & Multi-Modal Score Alignment
**Milestone:** `M7.3`  
**Status:** Certified Specification  
**Module:** `src/modules/engineering-library/retrieval`

---

## 1. Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) Model

Lexical and vector search return candidates on disparate scales. M7.3 applies Reciprocal Rank Fusion:

$$RRF(d) = \sum_{m \in \{\text{lexical}, \text{vector}\}} \frac{w_m}{k + \text{rank}_m(d)}$$

Where:
- $k = 60$ (smoothing constant preventing top ranks from dominating)
- $w_{\text{lexical}} = 1.0$
- $w_{\text{vector}} = 1.0$

---

## 2. Min-Max Score Normalization

To ensure standard output contracts, raw RRF scores are normalized to $[0, 1]$:

$$\text{NormalizedScore}(d) = \frac{RRF(d) - \min(RRF)}{\max(RRF) - \min(RRF)}$$

If only a single candidate exists, $\text{NormalizedScore} = 1.0$.

---

## 3. Advantages of RRF
1. **Scale-Independent:** Does not depend on the calibration of raw cosine similarities or lexical BM25 term frequencies.
2. **Dual-Match Prioritization:** Chunks ranked in both top-50 lexical and top-50 vector pools receive compounding scores, outranking single-mode hits.
3. **Outlier Resilient:** Extreme raw vector similarity values cannot distort the combined candidate list.
