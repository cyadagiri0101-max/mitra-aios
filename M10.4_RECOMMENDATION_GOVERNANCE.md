**Milestone:** `M10.4`

---

## 1. Recommendation Lifecycle

```
[GENERATED] ──► [REVIEW] ──┬──► [ACCEPTED] ──► [APPLIED]
                           ├──► [MODIFIED] ──► [APPLIED]
                           ├──► [REJECTED]
                           └──► [CANCELLED]
```
- Recommendations are strictly generated in `REVIEW` status.
- State transitions require authenticated user session and emit audit events.
