# M10.5 — Evidence Provenance Specification
## Provenance Classification & Non-Authoritative Invariant for AI
**Milestone:** `M10.5`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Provenance Categories

1. **TRANSACTIONAL_EVENT**: Authoritative relational mutations (Work orders, payments, BOM changes).
2. **HUMAN_EXPLICIT**: Direct user reviews, sign-offs, and approvals.
3. **IMPORTED**: Verified customer legacy assets.
4. **AI_INFERRED**: Leveling recommendations and predictions. **Marked non-authoritative**.
