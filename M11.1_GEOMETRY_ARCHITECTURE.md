## 1. Flow of Geometry & DFM Evidence

```
[CAD / STEP / Drawing Metadata]
              │
              ▼
   [Geometric Feature Service]
  (Unit normalization to mm, quality states)
              │
              ▼
 [Canonical Feature Persistence & EKOS Projection]
              │
              ▼
    [DFM Rule Engine Service]
  (Deterministic rule evaluation against standards)
              │
              ▼
 [Advisory DFM Findings (Status: OPEN)]
              │
              ▼
 [Governed Human Review: ACCEPT / MODIFY / REJECT]
```
