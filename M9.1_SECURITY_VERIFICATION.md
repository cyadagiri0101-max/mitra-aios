# M9.1 — Security & Multi-Tenant Verification
## Tenant Isolation, RBAC & Audit Trails
**Milestone:** `M9.1`  
**Status:** **PASSED & VERIFIED**

---

## 1. Security Invariants

1. **Strict Tenant Boundaries:** All database queries in `G14TelemetryService` and `G14FeatureEngineeringService` are scoped by `tenantId`. Cross-tenant IDOR attempts throw `NotFoundException` or `ForbiddenException`.
2. **RBAC Protection:** Endpoints under `/api/predictive/features` are protected with `JwtAuthGuard`, `RolesGuard`, and require `ADMIN` or engineering roles.
3. **Audit Logging:** Every extracted feature snapshot generates a structured audit log entry (`G14_FEATURE_SNAPSHOT_EXTRACTED`) recording project ID, cutoff date, and source hash.
