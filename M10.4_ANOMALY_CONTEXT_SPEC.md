## 1. Cycle-Time Variance Formulation

Let $T_i$ be the $i$-th observed cycle time signal, and $T_{\text{nominal}}$ be the standard reference:

$$\bar{T} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N T_i$$

$$\Delta_{\text{deviation}} (\%) = \frac{\bar{T} - T_{\text{nominal}}}{T_{\text{nominal}}} \times 100$$

If $\Delta_{\text{deviation}} > 15\%$, a `PROCESS_VARIANCE_CHECK` recommendation is raised with empirical telemetry evidence.
