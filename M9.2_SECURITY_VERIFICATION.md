## 1. Security & Governance Invariants

1. **Tenant Isolation:** Training and inference operations require an explicit `tenantId` and reject unauthenticated/unscoped callers (`ForbiddenException`). Cross-tenant dataset aggregation is strictly blocked.
2. **RBAC Guardrails:**
   - Model Training: Protected by `RolesGuard` and requires admin/engineering privileges.
   - Delay Inference: Protected by `JwtAuthGuard` and `RolesGuard`.
3. **Structured Audit Events:**
   - `G14_PREDICTIVE_MODEL_TRAINED`: Records model version, MAE, sample count, and provenance hash.
   - `G14_DELAY_PREDICTION_GENERATED`: Records project ID, predicted delay days, delay probability, and risk tier.
   - `G14_DELAY_PREDICTION_FALLBACK`: Records fallback invocation reason and cutoff.
