# M11.0 — Candidate Capabilities for Next-Gen Roadmap
## Evaluation of 6 Potential Strategic Directions
**Milestone:** `M11.0`  
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

---

## 1. Candidate Capability Descriptions

### Option A: Engineering Copilot 2.0 (Deep Engineering Reasoning)
- Multi-step reasoning across CAD metadata, drawing tolerances, material specs, and tooling physics.
- Automatic draft generation of DFM (Design for Manufacturing) insights and mold design reviews.

### Option B: Closed-Loop Manufacturing & What-If Simulation
- Governed discrete-event simulation of shop-floor machine allocations, mold trial schedules, and setup time minimization.
- What-if scenario analysis for plant managers before approving work order shifts.

### Option C: Enterprise Digital Thread & 3D Interactive Lineage
- Complete digital twin linking CAD 3D topology directly with shop-floor CMM inspection meshes, sensor heatmaps, and tool wear logs.
- Interactive 3D WebGL graph visualizer.

### Option D: Cross-Project Enterprise Portfolio Optimization
- Multi-plant capacity leveling, profit margin optimization across simultaneous RFQ bidding, and global supply chain risk aggregation.

### Option E: Governed AI Engineering Agents
- Autonomous research, document drafting, and engineering change proposal generation operating under strict multi-signature human approval.

### Option F: Enterprise Platform Productization & On-Prem Deployment
- Multi-region replication, automated backup snapshots, air-gapped container bundling, enterprise license management, and tenant observability.
