## 3. Provenance & Authority Metadata Preservation

Every ingested document retains:
- `sha256`: Cryptographic content identity fingerprint.
- `relativePath`: Exact repository vault path.
- `classification`: `ENGINEERING_DOCUMENT`, `MASTER_WORKBOOK`, `PARSER_SOURCE`, `ENGINEERING_DATA`.
- `authorityStatus`: `AUTHORITATIVE_RELEASE` or `CURRENT_WORKING`.
- `projectNumber`, `projectPrefix`, `customer`, `machine`, `material` (where discoverable).
- `tenantId`: Strict multi-tenant isolation.
