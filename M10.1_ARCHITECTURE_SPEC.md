### Core Invariants:
1. **Projection, Not Dual Source of Truth:** Bounded context entities (Projects, Drawings, Work Orders, NCRs) remain authoritative in their respective schemas.
2. **Deterministic Synchronization:** Node and edge registrations are strictly idempotent.
